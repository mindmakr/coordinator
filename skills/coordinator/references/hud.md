# The dashboard: a run at a glance

Three views of the same state, so the person never has to interrupt the loop to ask how it's going. All read `state.json` and nothing else.

## Text: `S/run.mjs show <run>`

About a dozen lines, for any harness and any terminal:

- **status, progress, eta** first: the run's status word (below), units done of total with a percentage and the round in progress, and the time left in plain words ("about 1 h 10 min left; ready around 21:40 local time", or "no estimate yet"). A concluded run says "This run is finished", why, what it delivered and its follow-ups in Gaps. The phase (`continue`, `integrate`, …) follows on its own line;
- **situation:** the lead's latest `note --kind situation`, or a count such as "3 of 6 tasks in progress, 1 done, 2 waiting";
- **needs you:** each open question with the lead's recommendation, or "nothing". `use-answer`, or `note --kind decision --answers q<N>`, closes question N;
- **answered:** each answer the lead has not used yet, and who gave it;
- **time left:** rough. The median minutes per try so far, times the tries still ahead on the longest chain of unfinished units, next to what the time budget has left;
- the budget used against its limits;
- one row per unit: status, round, score trend, next action and behaviours passing (`behaviours 1/2`); then its plain sentence, prefixed `[retrying]` or `[quiet]` when the lead is handling a problem, and its last gap;
- **goal:** met or not yet, with the goal's behaviours passing;
- the next step for the lead and the counts of questions, follow-ups and decisions.

`status` replies from it (`commands/status.md`).

## Status word, progress and ETA

One function, `runStatus` in `lib/progress.mjs`, computes them; `show`, `list`, `status-line`, the dashboard's header and the coordinator page all read it, so they never disagree (`test/progress.test.mjs` checks they match).

- **Running:** units being built or checked, or waiting their turn.
- **Waiting on you:** an open question, or a finish-line ask (`next` says `ask-person`).
- **Finishing:** every unit decided; the integration check or the close is left.
- **Concluded: done / partial / stopped:** the run is closed, with a one-line reason (the lead's early-close reason when there was one).

Progress counts passed and stopped units as done, out of all units. The ETA is the time-left estimate above, capped at the time budget, with the clock time it lands on in this machine's time zone. `S/run.mjs status-line [<run>]` prints the one line a lead posts (`commands/run.md`, Status in the chat); with no run, one line per run, active runs first, then the five most recently concluded.

## The live dashboard: `S/run.mjs serve <run> [--port P]`

A small server on 127.0.0.1 that renders the dashboard from the state on every request and keeps it current. It prints a URL with a token made fresh at each start; give it to the person once. Its rules are in `guardrails.md`.

- **Live, not reloading.** The page opens a Server-Sent Events stream (`/events`). When the state changes, and once a minute for the clocks, the server sends the page's content again, and the page updates itself in place (`lib/morph.mjs`): every row, card and section has a stable key, kept nodes stay, changed text and attributes are updated, and only new rows are inserted. Every key is unique on its page (unit ids are part of them), so the morph never confuses two rows. Because nodes are kept, the browser's own scroll anchoring (`overflow-anchor: auto`) keeps the reader's place; the page adds no scroll correction of its own. Every list that grows live (a unit's Evidence and behaviours, Decisions, Questions, Follow-ups, a unit's notes, the timeline) sits in its own anchor container (`.list-anchor`, `grows()` in `lib/parts.mjs`) whose tail, a box just under the list that takes no room, covers the padding and border its toggle and card leave below it; and cards in a row are only as tall as their content. So when the top of the screen sits just below a growing list, the browser anchors to something that moves with the list, not to a card or toggle whose top stays put (issue #23). It never swaps a block while one of its form fields has focus or holds typed text. A pill in the header shows the connection, in words as well as colour: **Live** (green, pulsing), **Reconnecting** (amber, pulsing; retries with backoff, 1 s doubling to 30 s), **Disconnected** (red: the server refused the page's token, which means it restarted, or five tries in a row failed; it then stops trying and says "if the server restarted, open the new link it printed", with a **Retry** button), **Finished** (foreground colour, the run is closed). A 15-second heartbeat lets it notice a silent drop. With reduced motion set, nothing pulses.
- **Kept state.** Open toggles (behaviours, evidence, gaps, notes), the scroll position after a reload (restored once images and fonts have loaded), the theme and the timeline filters are remembered per browser (localStorage, per run and page), across live updates and reloads.
- **Theme toggle:** System, Light or Dark, top right.
- **At the top:** the status block (the status word, units done with a progress bar, the round in progress and the ETA; on a concluded run a banner, "This run is finished", with the reason, what it delivered and its follow-ups in Gaps); the situation; **Goal: met / not yet**, with each goal behaviour, its status and evidence; **Needs you** with answer forms (options as cards, the recommended one first, an Other box); a note box; time left and the next step.
- **Who acts:** only **Needs you** and the question cards address the person. A unit that failed twice in a row shows **Retrying**, one whose agent went silent shows **Quiet**; both mean the lead is on it, with the reason on hover. A legend above the units says so.
- **Times** show in the viewer's locale and time zone: relative ("5 min ago") within 24 hours, the local date and time otherwise, and the full local time with its zone on hover. Each is a `<time datetime="…">` with the ISO timestamp; the filters read the raw timestamps.
- **Unit dots and state badges:** each card and unit page starts with a dot that says who the unit waits on: amber with a ring and a **Needs you** badge only when an open question on the unit waits for the person; hollow grey with **Waiting for A** (A linked) when it waits for another unit; blue with **Up next** when it is ready but not started; green and pulsing while building or being checked; violet while retrying; red when its agent is quiet; foreground when passed; grey when stopped. Every chip says the state in words (Working, Retrying, Quiet, Passed, Stopped, Waiting for A, Up next, Needs you; a unit both blocked and asked says **Needs you · waiting for A**), as do the dot's label and tooltip, the map tooltips and the self page's plans; the raw status chip is gone. The situation line counts the units not running by who they wait on: needs you, waiting for another unit, up next, with the lead. The card's text and Next line link the unit it waits for; the waited-on unit says **Blocks: D**; the unit page lists **Depends on / Blocks** with each unit's dot and badge. The Flow and Mind maps' legends use the same states. Active units come first; passed and stopped ones are dimmed, as are their timeline rows, and **Show finished** hides them (remembered per browser).
- **Tries and scores:** "Tries used: 2 of 4" with a bar (a try is one build plus one independent review; hover says so). It turns red only near the limit on a unit still running, and is grey once the unit is finished. "Score per try (0–10)" draws one dot per try, red for a failed try and green for a passed one; each dot is focusable and its tooltip says "Try 2: failed, 8/10 — <cause>". One try reads as words: "Passed on try 1 (9/10)". Budget cards explain themselves on hover; a full units bar is normal and not red. Once a unit has passed, an **escapes** card says how many defects turned up after a pass (`note --kind escape`), out of how many passed units, and lists them; `show` prints the same line when there are any.
- **Questions card:** open questions only, with their count. Answered ones (by the person, the lead, or a decision note that answers them) sit in a collapsed, dimmed **Answered** group: the question's header or first line, then "Answered: … — by you, 5 h ago" and the decision that closed it. On the timeline: "q1 answered: …".
- **Unit cards:** the plain sentence, issue and PR chips, the score per round, then toggles for **Behaviours** (each with pass, fail or unchecked), **Evidence** (test output in a monospace block, collapsed when long; screenshots as thumbnails that open in the evidence viewer; links), and the raw gap. **Open <unit> →** goes to the unit's page.
- **A unit's page** (`/unit/<id>`, also live): its bar and behaviours with their evidence, other evidence and artifacts, every round (the builder's report, the critic's verdict, score, cause, gap and evidence, and each behaviour mark), its questions and notes with forms, its links, and **its own timeline**: start, beats with their milestone, built, verdicts with score and cause, strategy changes, evidence, behaviours set, questions, answers and notes on the unit, links, merges and the stop. A back link returns to the dashboard.
- **Evidence viewer** (`lib/evview.mjs`, `lib/evview.client.js`): every stored screenshot, log or text file, and inline test output, opens in an overlay from the timeline (evidence rows link to it, screenshots with a small lazy thumbnail), the Evidence toggle and the unit page. It shows the item large in a scrolling area (tall full-page screenshots scroll), fit to width or actual size (the button, or click the image; pinch on a phone), Previous / Next with a count, the caption (unit, evidence id, label, kind, round, time), **Open in new tab** and **Download**. Text is escaped, in a monospace pane. PDFs open in a new tab (the page allows no frames). A stored copy that is gone shows **missing** and opens nothing. The URL hash names the open item (`#ev-<unit>-E4`), so a reload reopens it. On the live page Escape closes it and focus returns to the link; Tab stays inside; the arrow keys and a swipe step through the list it was opened from, skipping rows the timeline filter hides and following its order. Full screen below 640 px; no animation with reduced motion.
- **Timelines** (the run's on the dashboard, a unit's on its page): colour-coded badges by kind, each with its text label (decision, question, answer, note, verdict pass, verdict fail, stop, merge, evidence, strategy, behaviour, build, link, info). Above them: a search box over the event text; kind chips (several at once); unit chips on the run's timeline (`run` for run-level events); a time range (last 30 min, 120 min, 4 h, 8 h, 24 h; 2–7 days ago, meaning older than 24 h up to 7 days; 8+ days ago; All); and newest or oldest first. Search ignores case, accents and Arabic or Urdu diacritics, highlights every match in the visible rows (built from text nodes, so state text stays escaped) and shows "n matches"; highlights are cleared with the search and come back after each live update. The filter and the highlights are the same functions the tests check (`lib/timeline.mjs` `matches`, `lib/highlight.mjs`).
- **Links everywhere.** In any state text, http(s) URLs, `owner/repo#123`, and, against the run's repo, `#123`, commit shas and branch names (`feat/…`, `fix/…`) become links that open in a new tab with no referrer. Nothing else does. The run's repo is `init --repo owner/name`, else the project's `origin` remote on GitHub, else the repo most GitHub issue and PR links in the state point at.
- **Forms** go through the same locked code as `run.mjs answer` and `note --kind person`, so `watch` sees them within 2 seconds.

Stop it with Ctrl+C, or stop the background command, when the run ends.

**Why one script.** The page carries one inline script, allowed by a nonce made per response (`script-src 'nonce-…'`), plus `connect-src 'self'` for the stream and `img-src 'self'` for screenshots. Nothing else can run or load.

Content added inside the visible area moves what is below it, by design. Two cards side by side in a row can't both hold: when one grows above the top of the screen, its neighbour's content, beside it, stays where it is.

## The coordinator's page: `S/run.mjs self` and `/coordinator`

About the coordinator itself, across every run in the state folder. `S/run.mjs self` prints it as text (`--json` for the model, `--html` writes `coordinator.html` beside `runs/`); `serve` shows it live at `/coordinator`, linked from the header of every page (the header lists the served run and Coordinator; a new page adds itself to `pages` in `liveCtx` and to `PAGE` in `lib/serve.mjs`). One section per entry of `SECTIONS` in `lib/selfpage.mjs`:

- **Age:** first run, number of runs (and how many are open), units, hours at work (each run's start to its close or last event).
- **Active runs** (first): each open run's status word, units done with a small progress bar, the round in progress, its ETA and what needs you; above them when the machine frees up (the latest ready time among them, in local time); below, the five most recently concluded runs with their reason, what they delivered and their follow-ups in Gaps. `self` prints the same lines.
- **Plans:** each open run: its status word and progress, its phase, units finished, the next steps, and for each unit left what it waits on (its builder, a critic, the unit it stacks on, the lead, or you), and open questions.
- **Gaps:** everything left undone, from every run, grouped by run (newest first), so nothing is forgotten. The header, the nav entry and the section count the open ones ("12 open gaps across 6 runs"). Each row: kind, text (clipped; the full text behind a toggle), run and unit (linked), when it was raised, where it came from, and its id. Live: search and filter by run, kind and status, all remembered per browser like the open toggles. The search box filters as you type, ignoring case and accents, and matches the gap's text, run and unit ids, kind, source, id, resolution, URLs and refs typed as `#1230`, `1230`, `repo#1230`, `owner/repo#1230` or `PR #1230`; matches are highlighted as in the timeline search, the count reads "N shown · M matches highlighted", and Escape clears it. The snapshot has no search box. `S/run.mjs gaps [--all] [--json]` lists them. All rules below are closed lists (`lib/gaps.mjs`); nothing else counts.
  - **What shows:** stopped units (last gap and cause); units left neither passed nor stopped when their run closed; runs closed `partial` or `stopped` (the "closed early" reason); `follow-up` notes; follow-ups and deferrals that say *out of fence* or *outside the (run's) fence* (kind out-of-fence); `decision` and `info` notes that defer work, only with these phrases: *later* where a clause ends ("stays for later", "fix the wording later."), *follow-up issue(s)*, *deferred*, *defer to*, *next release*, *out of fence* (a hyphenated word such as "deferred-comment" does not count, nor "later disputes"); `upstream:` proposals in follow-up and retro notes (within a run, proposals naming the same files and functions, such as `board.mjs` and `url.match()`, count once; with none named, the same words; a note that records filing them, "posted upstream" or "upstream filed", is not a proposal); open questions; escapes.
  - **Resolved from the state:** a stopped unit with a `merge` link recorded after its last stop ("merged after stop"); a `decision` or `info` note after the unit's last verdict that opens with the unit id and says it merged ("m1 merged as PR #39"); a unit continued in a later run (`continues` / `continuedIn`) once the chain's last unit passed or its own gap is resolved (until then the row stays open and says **carried → run:unit**); a question answered or closed by a decision; a note naming a unit it was handed to ("folded into c12") once a unit of that id passed in that run or a later one; an upstream proposal whose run records the filing; a run closed early once every unit gap of it is. **Tracked refs:** a note, a stopped unit's last gap or a run's closing reason resolves only by an issue or PR ref that comes right after a tracking verb: *tracked*, *filed*, *opened*, *moved*, *logged*, *raised*, *continue(s|d)* (optionally with as, in, at, to or under, and "issue" or "PR"), or *issue is*: "moved to #42", "filed as acme/web#12", "the issue is acme/web#11". Any other mention, in any form and any repo, never counts: "see #1230 for context", "compare with …/pull/1230 behaviour", "listed in release notes on acme/web#1230". `#n` and `repo#n` resolve against the run's repo (`--repo`, else its unit links). A tracked ref in a repo no run's state names (its `--repo` or a unit link) does not resolve: the row stays open, marked **tracked (unverified)**, until `gaps --check` finds the ref exists. No network: a PR's merged state is not read.
  - **Closed by someone:** `S/run.mjs gap close <run> <id> --reason R` (the lead), or `--by person --confirm "<their words>"` (pasted, never without them), adds a decision note with `gap: <id>` to that run's log, closed runs too; `gap reopen` undoes it; the last note wins. Resolved gaps stay listed, folded, with the reason (clipped; the full reason on hover) and who closed it.
  - **Checked against GitHub (opt-in):** `S/run.mjs gaps --check [--run R] [--dry-run]` asks GitHub through `gh` (the gaps view's only network call) about two things only. A stopped or unfinished unit's own linked PRs: one merged closes the gap, "its PR <url> merged; the critic's last gap was not re-checked", and the merge commit is recorded as the unit's `merge` link. A gap tracked (unverified): the ref exists closes it, "tracked in <ref>, which exists (open|closed|merged)". A ref a note merely mentions is never asked. Each closure is a `gap close` decision note by the lead, "checked on <date>: …", with `checked: { at, ref, state }`; the page marks it "checked with gh" and says when the last check ran (`gaps-check.json` in the state folder). `--dry-run` lists what it would close and writes nothing; with no `gh`, or `gh` not signed in, it says so and writes nothing.
  - **Ids:** `<source>:<run>:<unit or ->:<seq>`, where source is the note's own kind (`follow-up`, `retro`, `decision`, `info`, `escape`) and seq its position in the run's notes, or `question` with `qN`, `stopped` with the unit's nth stop, `unfinished` or `run`. An id never depends on what the text says, so a closure holds when these rules change.
- **Working stats:** counted over all runs, open included (the page says so), with the closed-runs-only figures that `run.mjs stats` prints beside them: pass rate and stopped share of finished units, rounds to pass (median and p80), median time per unit (first build to last verdict), the suggested `--max-rounds`, and a table of the same per run (the trend).
- **Learning:** every lesson of both scopes, best first: tag, text, seen, used, **promote** once seen 3 or more times, the runs it came from (links) and its sources.
- **Memory:** `COORDINATOR.md` by section, and your answers from past runs (with the decision notes that closed questions).
- **History:** closed runs with outcome, units passed and stopped, start and close, each linking to its dashboard.
- **Proposals:** follow-ups, retro notes and `upstream:` notes from every run, and lessons ready to promote, newest first, linkified.
- **Side study:** the rule, each run's studies against its cap, and the study log with sources and lessons (`learning.md`).

- **This PC** (last): every run on this machine, from the run register (below), grouped by project with this one first and active runs first in each. Each run shows its status, the name the person uses for it, the tool and skill version that wrote it, and when it was last written; a run of another project links to its read-only pages at `/p/<key>/run/<run>`.

**The snapshot `coordinator.html`** in the state folder is rewritten after every change to any run of the folder, as `hud.html` is, so it is never older than the last change. It reads the folder's runs under its own lock (about half a second on a 39 MB folder) and lists the PC's runs from the register alone, without opening them. It says when it was written, and that the live page is `/coordinator:dashboard`. `self --html` rewrites it at once.

Run links go to `/run/<run>`: any other run of the folder, live and **read-only** (no forms; answer those on that run's own `serve`). Its unit pages are `/run/<run>/unit/<id>`. The page updates live when any run, lesson file or `COORDINATOR.md` changes. Tokens per unit are not recorded yet, so they are not shown.

## Every run on this PC: `/coordinator:dashboard` (`S/run.mjs dashboard [--port P] [--open] | dashboard stop`)

The global dashboard is for one developer on their own machine; the hosted service (`service/`) is the one for a team or the cloud. It serves, on 127.0.0.1 behind a per-start token, every run on this PC across projects and tools, grouped by project, each linked to its own read-only pages (`/p/<key>/run/<run>`, with `/unit/<id>`, `/maps`, `/report`, `/retro`; no forms; stored files stay on disk). Those pages read that project's own runs and lessons, and say "run `/coordinator:retro <run>` in <project>". `<key>` is a hash of the state folder, looked up in the register: a URL never names a folder. It updates live when the register or a registered run changes. A run's own `serve` serves the same `/p/…` pages, linked from its `/coordinator` page.

- **One per machine.** It listens on a stable port, 7480 (`--port P` to choose; when 7480 is taken and no port was named, a free one), and records `{ pid, port, url }` in `~/.coordinator/dashboard.json`, readable by you only, since the URL holds the token. Run it again while it runs and it prints that link instead of starting another, once the link answers. Both `dashboard` and `list --all` first register this folder's runs from before the register.
- `--open` opens the link in the browser. `dashboard stop` stops it by its recorded pid, only once the recorded link answers (its token proves the process is the dashboard); otherwise it drops the stale record and stops nothing.

**The run register** (`lib/registry.mjs`): `~/.coordinator/registry.json` (`COORDINATOR_HOME` moves the folder). Every write to a run records it: project folder, state folder, run id, title, the name the person uses (`init --alias`), the tool and skill version that last wrote it, the tool that started it, its outcome once closed, and when. Written atomically under a lock, so leads in different tools can write at once. Titles are masked like every other text, and nothing else in it is free text. Entries whose run folder is gone are dropped on every write and skipped on every read. A run from before the register appears after its next write (`run.mjs hud <run>` is enough). `S/run.mjs list --all` prints it by project, each run with its status line. `COORDINATOR_REGISTRY=off` leaves it alone.

**Where a run came from.** Every write stamps the run with the tool (`--harness`, else `COORDINATOR_HARNESS`, else detected: `harnesses.md`) and the skill version: `startedBy` once, at init, and `writer` on every event, as the event's last op, so a copy that predates the stamp still reads the log. The dashboard's header shows "started in Antigravity · skill 1.1.0", the last writer when it differs, and the `--alias` name beside the id. A run last written by another version shows a warning ("written by skill 1.0.0; this dashboard is 1.1.1") and loads as usual. A run from before the stamp says "tool and skill version not recorded".

## The maps: `/maps` and `maps.html`

Five pictures of one run, drawn as inline SVG from the same view model as the dashboard (`lib/maps.mjs` and `lib/heat.mjs` build the data, `lib/mapsvg.mjs` draws it). Live at `/maps` on `serve` (in the header, and **Maps →** on the dashboard; `/run/<run>/maps` for another run), and as `maps.html` beside `hud.html` in the snapshot. No script and no network; colours are theme classes, so light, dark and a project theme apply. Each map's box is focusable too (a named region with a focus ring), so a wide map can be scrolled from the keyboard on a phone. Every node is a link you can tab to, with its words in a tooltip and `aria-label`: a unit opens its page, the goal opens the dashboard, an issue or PR opens GitHub in a new tab. One section per entry of `MAPS` in `lib/mapspage.mjs`:

- **Mind map:** the goal at the centre, units on the first ring, their behaviours on the second, evidence outside (six per parent, then `+n`). Coloured by status: the unit's dot, a behaviour's pass, fail or unchecked.
- **Flow:** units in columns by stacking depth (`--after`), then the issues and PRs they link (one node per issue, so two units on one issue show it; another repo's are dashed), then follow-ups, attached to the unit they name, the note's unit, or an issue the unit links. **Critical path:** the stacked chain with the most work in it. A finished unit weighs its minutes worked (first start to last verdict); an unfinished one the median a finished unit took (30 min if none), or its minutes so far once past that. Drawn thick, with a line above saying how long and how many units are left. With no stacking it says so and draws no path. Follow-up lines are drawn above the issue nodes they cross. A unit that continues, or was continued in, another run (`init --continues`) links a dashed box for that run in the issues column, with a ring at the run end; the unit card, unit page (its earlier rounds under **Earlier runs**), stats panel and the coordinator page (**Work across runs**) show the same chain.
- **Activity by hour:** units against time in UTC, one column per hour since the start (wider columns past 48 hours). Darker means more happened (a try counts double, a heartbeat or other event once); red holds a failed try; amber is time waiting for a critic (built to verdict) or an answer, overlaps counted once. Hover a cell for its counts; a row's link says its totals and busiest hour.
- **Behaviours:** units against their behaviours (B1, B2, … by position, the goal's row first): pass ✓, fail ✗ or unchecked ·.
- **Goal relevance:** goal behaviours on the left, units on the right. A unit is **named** when its title, bar or behaviours name a goal behaviour's id (with no goal behaviours: the goal names the unit or an issue it links), **inferred** when they share three or more words, and **off goal** otherwise, grouped at the bottom with an amber dashed border.

**Labels and panels** (`lib/maptips.mjs`): every unit box shows a status mark (✓ pass, ✗ fail, ■ stopped, ○ open, ▶ building, ◆ judging; never colour alone) and its id, with the title clipped by an ellipsis and in full on hover. The mind map's ring grows with the unit count so neighbouring boxes never overlap. Hovering, focusing or tapping a unit's box, in any map, shows its panel: status, round n/max, the scores trail, time spent, the last gap and cause, linked issues and PRs, and files touched (`files` in the view model, from the gaming check's last diff count). The boxes it connects to and their edges stay lit; the rest dims. Stacked edges are labelled "waits on" and point to the unit waited for; issue and PR boxes name the units that link them, follow-ups the unit that filed them, goal behaviours the units that serve them. Maps are drawn at their own size, never shrunk, and scroll inside their box (a hint says so on a phone, or when wider than the page). The panel is a non-modal dialog. CSS shows it and does the dimming, so the snapshot works with no script; on the live page a small script places it clear of the box and what it lights, pins it on a tap (a second tap opens the unit), lets Tab enter it from its box and leave past its last link, and closes it on Escape or a tap elsewhere. The project map's boxes do the same with a module's files, helpers, surfaces, settings and import counts.

**Walking Flow and Goal relevance** (`lib/mapnav.mjs`, `lib/mapnav.client.js`): on these two maps, hovering or focusing a box lights it and its direct children only (Flow: the units stacked after it, the issues and PRs it links, the follow-ups it filed; Goal relevance: the units that serve a goal behaviour), never its parent or grandchildren. Every line is focusable and named ("m3 waits on m1"); hovering or focusing it lights its two ends. Its pointer target is two invisible strokes along its own path, drawn under every box (a box always wins inside itself): a wide layer, then a narrow one over all the wide ones, so a point on a line's centre reaches that line. Lines leaving or reaching one box spread along its side, in the order of where they go, and part at once. Follow-up lines are drawn again above the issue column but take no pointer. `test/browser/hits.check.mjs` (Playwright, outside `npm test`) checks every box centre and nine points along every line on real runs. CSS does this, so the snapshot has it. On the live page, a click, tap, Enter or Space selects a box instead of opening it: it stays lit with its children until clicked again, Escape or **Clear selection**, and the stats panel stays hidden meanwhile. A panel floating at the bottom left (so the map never moves) shows a breadcrumb back to a root, the box's words, **Open unit** (or Open issue, PR, follow-up, the dashboard), **More…**, **Up to parent** and its children as buttons; → goes to the first child, ↑ ↓ to the next or previous child, ← back up. Right-click, long-press, the Menu key or Shift+F10 opens a menu: open, open in a new tab, select children, copy a link. **Find** selects a box by id or title (Enter picks the first match); **Focus** hides what is not selected. Counts above each map say how many units, issues and PRs, follow-ups and lines it holds. Flow's line ends each mean one thing: a filled arrowhead points to the unit waited for (`--after`), blue on the critical path; a ring sits at the issue or PR a unit links; an open arrowhead points to a follow-up. Goal relevance has no arrowheads: lines run from a goal behaviour to a unit serving it, solid green when named, dashed when inferred.

**What survives a reload and a live update:** the selection (in the URL hash, `#sel=<map>/<keys>`, so a copied link opens on it, and in sessionStorage), Find and Focus, a pinned panel, scroll inside each map, open toggles, timeline filters, the page's scroll and the theme. The live update resets classes, so after each patch the page puts the selection and pin back (`coordinator:patched`). Storage blocked: everything works, nothing is remembered.

**Export PDF:** every page (dashboard, unit pages, maps, project map, coordinator page) has an **Export PDF** button on the live page that opens the browser's print dialog (choose Save as PDF); the snapshot runs no script, so it says "Ctrl+P or ⌘P, then Save as PDF". The print styles are in both: always the light theme, every toggle printed open (and closed again after), live controls, panels and menus hidden, maps scaled to the page width (at most 190 mm tall), cards, rows, maps and panels kept whole and headings kept with what follows where they fit, the page title at the top of every page and "Page n of m" at the bottom.

## The report and the retro: `/report` and `/retro`

Two more pages per run, in the header beside Maps (**Report →** and **Retro →** on the dashboard), live on `serve` and as `report.html` and `retro.html` in the snapshot:

- **Report:** the release notes the lead stored, for devops and testers; each unit with its issues, PRs and merges; migrations, env vars, scripts, jobs and copy awaiting approval read from git; and the code quality and compliance signals. `quality.md` says how each part is made.
- **Retro:** the run's retro notes, the lessons it added (both scopes), its budget against the suggested `--max-rounds` from closed runs with the same tag, the units stopped and why, upstream proposals, and escapes with their units.
- **Retro pending:** once every unit has passed or stopped (or the run reached done, done with gaps or stopped) and no `note --kind retro` exists, the dashboard and the retro page show a banner with the command, `/coordinator:retro <run>`. It goes once a retro note is logged.

## The snapshot: `hud.html` and `units/<unit>.html`

`.coordinator/runs/<run>/hud.html`, `maps.html`, `report.html`, `retro.html` and one page per unit under `units/` are rewritten after every change, in the same lock as the state. `S/run.mjs hud <run>` rewrites them on demand.

- They are read-only snapshots: no scripts, no network, no reload (its content security policy blocks all three), so they open from `file://` and can be published as a page. Open questions show their options; the page says to answer in the chat or ask for the live dashboard.
- The theme toggle works without a script there but is not remembered; toggles start closed. Times are ISO with "UTC" spelled out.
- Screenshots up to 150 KB are embedded in the Evidence lists; the timeline thumbnails and the evidence viewer load the files under `artifacts/` by relative path (`img-src 'self'`). The viewer works without a script: it is the element the URL hash names, with Close, Previous and Next as links.

## Looking like the project

The dashboard can take its colours, fonts, radius and spacing from the project's design system: `init --theme <file.css>`, or a `Theme: <path>` line in `COORDINATOR.md` (relative to the project). The CSS file is read, never loaded:

- custom properties from `:root`, `@theme`, daisyUI theme blocks, and dark ones from `.dark`, `[data-theme=dark]`, `prefers-color-scheme: dark` or a theme named dark;
- `--background`, `--card`, `--foreground`, `--muted-foreground`, `--border`, `--primary`, `--primary-foreground`, `--success`, `--warning`, `--destructive`, `--radius`, `--font-sans`, `--font-mono`, `--spacing`, also as `--color-*`, and daisyUI's `--color-base-100/200/300/content`, `--color-error`, `--radius-box`. `var()` is resolved within the file; shadcn's bare `222 47% 11%` becomes `hsl(…)`;
- a value that is not a plain colour or length, or holds `url(`, `@`, braces or angle brackets, is dropped. A font set at runtime (`var(--font-geist-sans)`) is dropped and the rest of the list kept. Anything missing keeps the built-in theme; a project without dark values keeps the built-in dark.

The Coordinator mark stays in the foreground colour, so it fits any theme.

## The project map

When the run has a map (`S/run.mjs map <run>`), the header links **Project map →** to `map.html` in the snapshot and `/map` (or `/run/<run>/map` for another run) on the live dashboard: the import graph with its filters, surfaces, settings, shared helpers and reuse candidates (`map.md`).

## Hearing the person: `S/run.mjs watch <run>`

Polls the state every 2 seconds and prints one line per new or changed answer and per note from the person, then keeps going:

```
answer q2 (u1) by person: No · Merge
note by person on u3: Check the Arabic labels
```

At start it prints the answers still waiting for the lead. `S/run.mjs questions <run>` prints every question with its options, latest answer, history and status.
