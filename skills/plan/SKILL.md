A built-in sub-skill of the coordinator. Read and follow `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../coordinator/references/sub-skills/plan.md`, and the shared rules in `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../coordinator/references/sub-skills.md`. Outside Claude Code, `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}` is this skill's folder.
